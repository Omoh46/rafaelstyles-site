Category Two

Artisanal Enhancements & Upgrades

✦

Add-on enhancements only, always paired with a SIGNATURE EXPERIENCE above, never booked standalone.

Add-On Enhancement

High-Definition Thermal Finish

Upgrade your blowout using premium hot tools to create cascading waves, luscious curls, or a detailed, smooth glass-like flat iron finish.

Add-On Enhancement

The Bombshell Brush-Set

The ultimate master technique for dramatic volume. Multiple premium round brushes are left locked directly within the hair to cool and set naturally as a luxury roller-set system, creating unparalleled body, volume, and movement with flawless flare and bounce.

Add-On Enhancement

Red-Carpet Roller-Clip Retention

A vintage setting technique for maximum volume longevity. Every section is meticulously sculpted into a pin-curl and anchored to the scalp using premium silver duck-bill clips upon round-brush release to lock maximum volume in place. These clips are left in the hair to set and are yours to keep as you retain your volume until it’s time for your future reveal.

Add-On Enhancement

Tailored Bespoke Accents

Soft, poetic styling enhancements including chic ponytails, organic braids, undone buns, or custom accessory placement.

Note: focuses on soft, effortless, classic finishes; architectural updos are NOT YET AVAILABLE.

Add-On Enhancement

Luxe Extension Integration

Meticulous placement, secure anchoring, and expert heat-styling/blending of your personal extensions into your finished blowout style. Guests provide their own fresh, completely clean, bone-dry, and thoroughly detangled clip-in extensions or premium halo wefts only.

Add-On Enhancement

The Coiled Satin Alignment (Silk Press)

(Silk Press) Elite thermal smoothing and precision silk press for highly coiled or deeply textured hair.

Add-On Enhancement

Bespoke Tress Liberation

A specialized, highly restorative detangling session designed to gently release intricate knots and stubborn matting without structural breakage using premium slip-agents.

Services for dreadlocks are not available.

Add-On Enhancement

Extended Care

A highly recommended scheduling buffer if your hair features exceptional length, rich texture, or magnificent density that routinely requires a little extra styling time to safeguard our service timeline.

The Rafael Styles Courtesy: this buffer is only charged if actually used, if your service completes within the standard block, the fee is waived at checkout.
